## Analysis

Subsystems affected: renderer (UBO tail), maps, **Prop (Phase E opens)**.

* 381–398: the light-UBO repair tail — see chunk 19. 389 "simple_map_test"
  updates `maps/Simple_Map_Test.json`.
* 388 "Fix terrain and shadow GL program state" — terrain and shadow passes had
  been left bound to the wrong program after the UBO change.

**399/400 open the Prop physics transformation** ("Unify Prop collision and
physics into one Physics control", "Place unified Prop Physics section
correctly"). What existed before: `no_collision` and `physics_enabled` were
independent authored flags. 399 merges them into one control — and this is
**reverted five commits later** at 404–407 ("Allow independent Prop physics and
collision controls", "Restore independent Prop collision state", "Respect
independent Prop collision setting", "Test independent Prop physics and
collision settings").

So: a data-model change was made, propagated into the editor (400), made
authoritative in the runtime (401/402), tested (403) — and then entirely undone
over the next four commits, with its own test replaced. Nine commits, net zero,
on the authored meaning of two Prop properties.

Compatibility note: because 399–403 changed how `no_collision` was interpreted
and 404–407 changed it back, **any map saved by a build between those commits
could carry a collision flag written under the merged semantics.** The range is
short and unreleased, so the practical risk is nil, but it is the clearest
example in this history of a data-format change landing before the design was
settled.
